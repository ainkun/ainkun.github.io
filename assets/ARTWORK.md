## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-width background illustration for a Japanese anime-inspired cybersecurity portfolio. Create an exceptionally beautiful cinematic panoramic anime cyber-city at blue hour after rain, a layered Tokyo-inspired futuristic skyline seen from an elevated rooftop, intricate hand-painted urban buildings, small cyan and magenta neon signs, electric cables and railway tracks leading into the distance, luminous violet clouds, a warm peach pink horizon, atmospheric perspective and wet reflections. Rich Japanese animation background art, fine architectural linework, painterly sky, sophisticated muted indigo and dusty lavender with coral pink and icy cyan lights. Wide 16:9 landscape composition; keep the middle-left sky relatively quiet and dark for large white website typography, place the most detailed neon city architecture along the lower half and right third. Entire image must be a cohesive detailed city landscape, not a mockup or UI. No people, no anime characters, no logos, no watermarks, no large readable lettering. Generate a high-quality landscape image.
