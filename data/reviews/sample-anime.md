## How a review is written

Reviews are written in Markdown, using a few simple marks:

- `**bold**` for **bold** and `*italic*` for *italic*
- `## Heading` starts a new section
- `> quote` sets a quote apart
- `[text](https://example.com)` makes a link
- `![Alt text](assets/reviews/picture.webp "Caption")` on its own line adds a picture
